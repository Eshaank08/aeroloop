A build challenge. Two days, a domain of your choosing, judged on what actually runs at the end.

Software improves fast because it has a loop: write, run, test, fix. That loop is why Devin can work unsupervised. Every attempt gets a verdict, and a wrong answer announces itself. Most industries have the same engineering problems and no such loop. The work goes out, and someone finds out months later whether it was right.

Your job is to find an industry that does have the loop, and build the layer that puts Devin inside it. Pick a domain where the output can be expressed as code. Then build a system that triggers Devin sessions programmatically, gives them a way to check their own work, and produces real artifacts with nobody sitting in the middle.

Pick a domain with no way to check the work and Devin is only guessing. Leave a human in the loop and you've built a copilot, not a layer. Wire it to one prepared example and you've built a demo.

Every industry has an engineering problem. Give it an engineer.Judging CriteriaWhat you built:

- Autonomy: Trigger to artifact with nobody touching it in between
- Verification: The system knows a good result from a bad one on its own
- Artifacts: Output someone in that industry could actually use

How you pitch it:

- Clarity: If we can't follow it, we can't credit it
- Problem: Which industry, who's stuck, and why it has a feedback loop
- Approach: The strategy you went in with, and how it changed as you built
- Solution: What you ended up with, and the logic behind it
