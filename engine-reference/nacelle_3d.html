Real, documented defect locations (red zones):
1 — Fan blade root fatigue crack. Southwest Flight 1380 (2018, fatal), CFM56-7B. Low-cycle fatigue crack present since a 2012 overhaul; missed by fluorescent penetrant (surface) inspection, only catchable by eddy-current. NTSB / Flight Safety Foundation.
2 — Fan cowl latch, underside. 45+ incidents, Airbus A320-family (CFM56-5B/V2500 — a related but different engine family from the -7B modeled here). Latches sit low and underneath, a zone routine walk-arounds and fixed-angle cameras structurally struggle to reach. Aviation Safety Network.
3 — Inlet lip / fan cowl acoustic panel. Composite facesheet disbond, core crush, water ingress — invisible to plain visual inspection, needs flash thermography at a stable, perpendicular standoff per panel. ASTM E2582 / nacelle MRO literature.
