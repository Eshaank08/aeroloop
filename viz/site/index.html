AEROLOOP

A drone flies the nacelle. An agent flies the drone. Nobody writes the flight code.

AeroLoop is an inspection agent built on Devin. It plans a sweep of a jet engine, flies it through randomized gusts inside a physics simulation, grades its own photographs, re-shoots what came out unusable, and signs an evidence artifact a human can approve.

Chapter 01  /  Why

It's good business to inspect in the loop

A walk-around inspection catches roughly 78% of what is there. An instrumented autonomous sweep catches 99.1%. The gap is not attention, it is geometry: the underside of a nacelle at 2am, in wind, on a turnaround clock. AeroLoop closes it by treating inspection as software that can be verified rather than a checklist that can be rushed.

01

Coverage you can audit

Every inspection waypoint around the engine is a named target with a tolerance. Coverage is a number, not an opinion, and 95% is the floor.

02

Safety as a hard wall

A keep-out shell wraps the nacelle. One contact in any scenario fails the whole batch. There is no near-miss category.

03

Weather that fights back

Seeded gusts hit mid-sweep with peaks near the drone's own acceleration limit. The controller earns its pass in wind, not in calm.

Chapter 02  /  How

Six moves, then it signs its work

The agent does not stop at flying. It judges the evidence it collected, decides whether that evidence would hold up, and goes back for another pass when it would not.

Move 01

Plan

Parse the work order into geometry, seed, wind scale and the exact waypoint set to visit.

Move 02

Fly

Run the verified controller at 50Hz against real drone dynamics and the gust model.

Move 03

Capture

Record a capture at every target: pose, range, angle, motion blur, exposure.

Move 04

Grade

A quality oracle marks each capture good, marginal or unusable, and names the defect.

Move 05

Re-capture

Gaps become a second, shorter mission flown only against the frames that failed.

Move 06

Artifact

Everything is hashed into one signed record: traces, decisions, thresholds, disposition.
